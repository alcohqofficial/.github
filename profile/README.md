## How we work

**Flat pricing.** The incentive to fix a recurring problem should sit with us,
not with the person paying by the hour.

**Documented, not memorised.** If a client leaves us, they leave with working
documentation. Lock-in through ignorance is not a retention strategy.

**Evidence over adjectives.** We align our controls to HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS,
CMMC and NIST CSF, and we can show you how. We are careful to say *aligned to*
rather than *certified in* — a claim you cannot evidence is a liability at the
first security review, and it is trivially checkable.

## What lives here
